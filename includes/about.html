ABOUT BALUMUKA

Celebrating African Culture Through Music & Dance

OUR STORY

Balumuka Events was born from a passion for African music, dance, and culture. For over 13 years, we've been creating spaces where people come together to celebrate, connect, and experience the richness of African heritage.

What started as intimate gatherings has grown into one of Prague's most vibrant cultural movements, bringing together thousands of music and dance enthusiasts from all backgrounds.

From Afrobeats parties to Kizomba festivals, from intimate meet-ups to large-scale cultural celebrations, we create experiences that transcend borders and bring people together through the universal language of music and dance.
